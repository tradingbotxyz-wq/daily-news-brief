# Markets brief — 7 November 2026

## Top line
Wall Street steadied on Friday after Thursday's AI-chip rout, but Asia and Europe took the hit. Indian shares rallied for a seventh straight session on record foreign buying.

## World markets
| Index | Close | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Dow Jones | 51,286 | −0.46% |
| S&P 500 | 7,377 | +0.31% |
| Nasdaq | 24,579 | +1.09% |

- **AI selloff:** The chip index fell 12% on Thursday, its worst day since 2020, on fears the new Democrat-controlled Congress will curb AI data centres. It rebounded about 4% on Friday.
- **Asia:** Kospi fell as much as 8.8% (trading halted), Taiwan's Taiex closed −4.5%, and Japan's Nikkei slid about 7% intraday.
- **Europe:** STOXX 600 fell as much as 1.6% intraday, but is still up more than 10% in 2026.
- **US jobs:** Payrolls rose by 165,000 in October and unemployment fell to 4.1%, raising the odds of a December Fed hike.

## Company highlights
- **AMD** +10.5% and **Nvidia** +3.3% on the rebound.
- **Airbnb** rose after hours on an earnings beat and higher guidance.
- **Atlassian** −16% on weak growth; **Natera** −15% after a sudden CEO change.
